Workshop Categories#

The workshop encourages submissions spanning a number of categories:

  • Long papers, either reporting on completed work or offering a polemic discussion on a burning issue (up to 10 pages).
  • Short papers describing work in progress (up to 5 pages).
  • Demos (2 page abstract describing the demo)
  • Position Statement(2 page abstract establishing a research position).
  • Late breaking results (2 page abstract reporting the results).

All submissions may also use as many additional pages as are necessary for references.

All accepted submissions will be archived locally on this site. We intend to also submit a volume of long & short papers to be archved as workshop proceedings through CEUR. Demos & posters will only be archived locally.

Contribution Style#

For final proceedings submission it is mandatory to use the CEUR styles specifically the single column style from this page. It is also highly recommended that you prepare your submission for initial review using this style to avoid challenges later.

  • For LaTeX users: Use the Overleaf CEUR LaTeX template or download the zip archive for offline LaTeX compilation.
  • For Open Document Format users: ODT Template
  • For Word Users: DOCX Template

Pre-Proceedings Publication#

We’ve gotten into the habit recently of publishing pre-proceedings shortly before the workshop so that folk can attend having prepared for the workshop, knowing what to expect, and being ready to engage with the presented work.

So we intend, time permitting, to publish proceedings for CMNA 26 through CEUR Workshop Proceedings as has happened in some previous years, but should there be a delay, we’ll pre-publish papers here for use during the workshop.
